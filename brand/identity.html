Mark

The shield

The D turns on its side and becomes a shield. The inner lines echo the cast ridges on Igbo-Ukwu bronze and the layers of material inside every part.

Full mark

32 px and up

Use it at 32 px and larger: the logo, signage, documents, product plates and the website.

Small mark

Below 32 px

Below 32 px the two ridges blur into one smudge. This version keeps a single thicker ridge, so favicons, browser tabs and small icons stay sharp.

Tagline

Say what you do

"Strength, recovered" asks people to decode it. Terra Industries does the opposite. Each of its lines says what the company does and who it serves.

The Terra pattern

Action word, then what you do, then who or where it serves. No wordplay. A stranger gets it in one read.

From waste to strength

Source

It comes from your vision line, "Turning Recycled Materials into Industrial Capability." Same idea, fewer and plainer words.

Double meaning

"Industrial strength" means very tough. It also means the strength of a country's industry. Both fit Dike.

The name

Dike means strong. Igbo speakers hear the name inside the line. Everyone else still gets a clear sentence.

  • Building Africa's Industrial Strength

    Closest to Terra's regional framing. Pick this if Africa is your first market.

  • Turning Waste into Hardware

    Shortest and most literal. "Hardware" can read as computer parts to some people.

  • Turning Recycled Materials into Industrial Capability

    Your vision doc line. Accurate, but long, and "capability" is hard to picture.
