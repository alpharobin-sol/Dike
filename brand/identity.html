Mark

The shield

The D turns on its side and becomes a shield. The inner lines echo the cast ridges on Igbo-Ukwu bronze and the layers of material inside every part.

64 · 48 · 32 · 24 · 16 px

One mark at every size. At 16 px the shield outline and bronze color do the work. Most phones and laptops draw a 16 px icon with 32 real pixels, and there the ridges stay clear.

Tagline

Vision and mission in one line

Three short beats: where Dike builds, what it builds from, who it builds for. Africa first, then the world.

Headline

Engineered in Africa. Made from Waste. Built for the World.

What the first lines gave us

  • Engineered from Waste. Built for Performance.

    Kept the two-beat build and the word "engineered."

  • Turning Recycled Materials into Industrial Capability.

    Kept the idea of waste becoming capability. Cut the length.

  • Africa's Industrial Strength. Built for the World.

    Kept Africa first and "Built for the World."

  • Strength, recovered. Built brave.

    Dropped. Both need the backstory to make sense.

One wording rule

Keep Africa next to strength or engineering, never next to waste. A line like "Turning Africa's waste into…" can read as if Africa is a dumping ground. The headline passes: Africa sits with "engineered."

  • Engineered from Waste. Built for Performance.

    Your vision doc's line. Strong on mission and quality, with no Africa or global signal.

  • Africa's Industrial Strength. Built for the World.

    Round 4. Africa first and global, but it says nothing about waste or engineering.

  • Engineered in Africa. Built for the World.

    Shorter. Drops waste, so the mission line has to carry it.
