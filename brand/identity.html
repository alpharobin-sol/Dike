Mark

The shield

The D turns on its side and becomes a shield. The inner lines echo the cast ridges on Igbo-Ukwu bronze and the layers of material inside every part.

64 · 48 · 32 · 24 · 16 px

One mark at every size. At 16 px the shield outline and bronze color do the work. Most phones and laptops draw a 16 px icon with 32 real pixels, and there the ridges stay clear.

What the earlier lines gave us

  • Engineered from Waste. Built for Performance.

    Kept the structure. Swapped "performance" for "the future." Performance is a spec. The future is a mission.

  • Turning Waste into Industrial Strength.

    Kept as the strongest alternative. "From waste to strength" survives as the sign-off.

  • Engineered in Africa. Made from Waste. Built for the World.

    Dropped the region. Kept the idea that each part of the line does one job.

  • Strength, recovered. Built brave.

    Dropped. Both need the backstory to make sense.

  • Turning Waste into Industrial Strength.

    Global, plain, and carries the meaning of Dike. Less forward-looking.

  • Building the Future from What the World Throws Away.

    The manifesto as the tagline. Boldest, closest to SpaceX and Tesla, but long for a business card.

  • Engineered from Waste. Built for Performance.

    Your vision doc's original. Strong on quality, with no sense of where the company is headed.
